# Cereja Knowledge System integration

Repository: `eusouakell/cereja-knowledge-system`

## Responsibility

Cereja Knowledge System remains authoritative for:

- Núcleo — meaning, evidence, thesis, worldview and canonical knowledge;
- Flame — visual identity, design system, motion, accessibility and UI patterns.

## Factory relationship

Agents consume domain contracts from Núcleo and Flame.

They do not become the authority over those systems.

Examples:
- Flame UI Composer executes under Flame;
- Motion Web Director proposes motion under Flame;
- Editorial Typography Director proposes type-system changes under Flame;
- Research Synthesist may supply evidence to Núcleo but cannot declare a thesis canonical;
- Inclusive Experience Reviewer may flag risks but cannot silently rewrite brand policy.

## Human gates

Canonical changes to:
- brand;
- typography;
- motion families;
- knowledge authority;
- thesis state

remain human-approved in the domain repository.
