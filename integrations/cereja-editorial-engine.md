# Cereja Editorial Engine integration

Repository: `eusouakell/cereja-editorial-engine`

## Responsibility

Cereja Editorial Engine remains authoritative for:

- Editorial Packet;
- content tokens;
- format composition;
- channel evals;
- editorial gates;
- publication approval.

## Factory relationship

The Factory supplies bounded capabilities.

Examples:
- Research Synthesist can assemble evidence;
- visual specialists can produce proposals/artifacts;
- Accessibility Auditor can independently review digital surfaces;
- Production Readiness Evaluator can summarize evidence.

They do not replace:
- thesis approval;
- editorial intent;
- rights approval;
- final publication decision.

## Publication boundary

No Factory agent has autonomous publish authority.

Editorial publication remains a human gate owned by the Editorial Engine workflow.
