## Single dominant visual idea

### System credential

Treat a first-class agent as a **credentialed actor inside a system**.

The visual anchor is one oversized physical access credential / system badge entering the composition at an assertive crop. It should feel like an editorial object, not a SaaS card component.

The badge carries a small set of system fields — identity, authority, state and audit — as evidence of the concept. The headline and badge must occupy the same depth system rather than sit in separate layout slots.

## Composition direction

- Large headline begins as the main editorial statement, with one portion passing behind or being occluded by the badge.
- Badge enters from the lower/right field at an optical angle created by crop/perspective, **not** by randomly rotating a UI card.
- A real-feeling strap, slot, punched edge, stamped field or registration mark may create materiality; use one or two, not a scrapbook kit.
- The badge should create one strong foreground/background relationship with type.
- Supporting concepts appear as small credential fields or registration metadata, not four decorative pills.
- Leave a deliberate quiet area so the object/headline relationship has room to work.

## Type × image

- Type is part of the scene, not a caption above an illustration.
- The headline may be interrupted by the credential only where the sentence remains unmistakably legible.
- Use display scale with strong optical hierarchy; do not use generic giant-serif-plus-neutral-sans as the entire identity.
- Small system fields use a functional/meta voice and contrast against the tactile object.

## Color / materiality

- Canvas: warm white / paper-like neutral.
- Ink: Flame ink.
- Cherry: embedded in the credential/strap/stamp or one meaningful intervention.
- Lime: one authorization/status material detail if it helps the credential metaphor.
- Do not create a full cherry/lime template or use both colors merely to prove brand compliance.
- Texture should come from the object/material, not a global grain filter.

## Deliberate omissions

- no robot, android, glowing brain or chat bubble;
- no purple/blue AI gradient;
- no cherries by obligation;
- no brand flame unless the composition needs subtle ownership after the concept works;
- no paper scraps, tape, doodles and stickers accumulated as a style checklist;
- no #031 layout inheritance;
- no carousel continuity problem;
- no Kell portrait in this test.

## Main risk

The credential metaphor can collapse into a generic conference badge, cyber-security poster or UI card. The composition must feel editorial and specific through scale, crop, type/object integration and material treatment — not through iconography.

## Evaluation questions

1. Does this feel like senior art direction rather than a written list of effects?
2. Is there one dominant compositional idea?
3. Do type and object share depth?
4. Does the brand feel present without a decorative asset checklist?
5. Could this belong to almost any AI/startup account after changing colors?
6. Is the metaphor immediately understandable without becoming literal/corporate?
7. Is the direction specific enough for a separate designer to execute without inventing it?
